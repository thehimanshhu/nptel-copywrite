### Conference Home

### About

As part of our ongoing efforts to promote academic excellence and research dissemination, NPTEL offers Conference Sponsorship Support to local chapter SPOCs/faculty members, and NPTEL STARs. This initiative enables them to present their research findings at reputed national conferences, thereby fostering collaboration,
innovation, and professional growth.

### Objectives

* Encourage presentation of high-quality research at recognized conferences.
* Support capacity building and professional development among faculty and students.
* Promote institutional visibility and research outreach across platforms.

### Who Can Apply

Applicants must fulfill the following conditions:

* Must be a local chapter SPOC/faculty member, or NPTEL STAR.
* The paper must be accepted for oral or poster presentation at a recognized conference.
* The applicant should be the primary or presenting author.
* The institution must endorse the application through the designated authority (e.g., Head of Institution / SPOC).
* Applicants must not have received conference sponsorship from NPTEL in the same academic year.
* All participants sponsored by NPTEL must acknowledge its support in any presentation, publication, or material related to the sponsored event.

### Nature of Support

Sponsorship covers the following:

* Conference registration fee.
* Travel and accommodation expenses (as per organization norms).

The final amount sanctioned will depend on the nature of the conference, location, and availability of funds.

### Documents Required

Applicants are required to submit

* Conference invitation / acceptance letter confirming paper presentation.
* Conference brochure or official website link.
* Copy of the accepted abstract / full paper.
* Endorsement / recommendation from Head of Institution or designated authority.

Incomplete applications or those missing required documentation will not be processed.

### Application Process

* Submit the online sponsorship application form.
* Upload all required documents in PDF format.
* Submit copy of the accepted abstract / full paper.
* The proposals will be reviewed by the Central Committee.
* Applicants will be informed of the decision by email.

### Post-Conference Requirements

Within 15 days of the conference, sponsored participants must submit:

* Participation certificate / proof of presentation.
* Travel and expense receipts (if reimbursement-based).
* A summary report explicitly acknowledging the role of NPTEL in the work or paper presented.

Failure to submit post-event documents may result in ineligibility for future support.

### Important Notes

* Applications without requisite documents will be automatically rejected.
* Decisions made by the evaluation committee are final and binding.
* The organization reserves the right to modify or withdraw the scheme at any time based on policy and fund availability.

### FAQ

### General Queries

1. What is the purpose of NPTEL providing support for conference participation?

   NPTEL aims to empower SPOCs/STARs through professional development opportunities by sponsoring participation in reputed academic and technical conferences. Such events foster networking,
   exposure to new research, and engagement with experts. The insights gained are expected to benefit the home institution and strengthen the NPTEL community.

2. Who identifies the conference to attend?

   SPOCs/STARs are expected to identify relevant conferences within their discipline. NPTEL does not maintain or recommend a fixed list. However, the selected event must align with NPTEL’s
   venue and academic quality guidelines.

3. Why are the supported conference venues restricted?

   Support is currently extended only to conferences hosted by reputed institutions such as IITs, IISc, IISERs, IIST, NITs, and CSIR labs, to ensure academic credibility and relevance.
   Conferences organized by Management or Humanities departments at these institutions are also eligible.

4. What is the significance of the IEEE Conference on Technology for Education (T4E)?

   The IEEE T4E Conference focuses on innovations in teaching, learning, and education technology. It is a recommended forum for SPOCs/STARs interested in sharing research or experiences
   related to technology-enabled learning. Submissions typically open in July, with results announced by August–September.

5. Are non-teaching SPOCs eligible for this support?

   Yes. Non-teaching staff serving as SPOCs are fully eligible for NPTEL’s conference support initiative.

6. Can another faculty member attend the conference on behalf of the SPOC?

   No. The sponsorship is non-transferable. Only the approved SPOC can avail the support.

7. Are faculty members from the Local Chapters (other than the SPOC) eligible?

   Yes, provided they obtain approval from the Head of Institution and apply through the SPOC, who must submit the conference support form on their behalf.

8. How many conferences can be supported per SPOC/STAR in a year?

   Each SPOC/STAR can receive support for one conference per academic year.

9. Is there a monthly or semester-wise limit on the number of requests supported?

   Yes. Applications are reviewed monthly, and only a limited number of SPOCs/STARs are sponsored in each cycle, depending on available funds and merit.

10. Is the support applicable for a limited time period?

    Yes. The initiative is reviewed annually, and its continuation depends on the outcomes and feedback from supported participants.

11. How and when is the support amount disbursed?

    The approved amount is reimbursed after participation. SPOCs/STARs must submit all relevant bills, certificates of attendance, and required documentation.

12. What expenses are covered under this support?

    The financial support covers:
    • Conference registration fees
    • To-and-fro travel expenses
    • Accommodation during the conference

    The total support is capped at ₹25,000. Personal or non-conference-related expenses are not covered.

13. Can SPOCs/STARs choose any topic for their conference paper?

    Yes, as long as it aligns with the conference’s theme and is accepted through its peer-review process. NPTEL does not impose topic restrictions.

#### Queries Related to the Application Form

14. Where can SPOCs find their College LC ID?

    The LC ID can be found on the official NPTEL Local Chapter page. A direct link is provided in the application form.

15. How do I enter the conference website URL?

    Enter the complete and correct URL of the conference homepage. Avoid incomplete or shortened links.

16. Do I need to acknowledge NPTEL’s support?

    Yes. All sponsored participants must acknowledge NPTEL’s support in their presentation, paper, or report.

17. Is a post-conference report required?

    Yes. SPOCs must submit a brief report summarizing key learnings, professional outcomes, and the overall benefit to their institution and the NPTEL community.
