## Details on SWAYAM-NPTEL Local Chapter

> **[TABS]** Two tabs: "List of SWAYAM-NPTEL Local Chapter" (open by default) and "Rating of Local Chapter". On narrow screens the tab labels are shortened to "LC List" and "Rating". The open tab is chosen by the query string and clicking a tab updates it in the address bar without reloading: https://nptel.ac.in/localchapter/details (no query string), https://nptel.ac.in/localchapter/details?page=lclist and https://nptel.ac.in/localchapter/details?page=LCList all open the first tab; https://nptel.ac.in/localchapter/details?page=ratings opens the second tab.
>
> | List of SWAYAM-NPTEL Local Chapter | Rating of Local Chapter |
> | --- | --- |

#### List of SWAYAM-NPTEL Local Chapter

> **[QUERY STRING]** Shown for the page address with no query string, ?page=lclist or ?page=LCList (the live site's sidebar and the Local Chapters page link to ?page=LCList).

> **[SEARCH BOX]** Text box with a search icon and the placeholder "search". Typing filters the table as you type, matching any part of the college name, LC Id, address, city, pincode, state, SPOC name or co-ordinated-by value, ignoring case, and returns to page 1.

> **[DROPDOWN]** Rows per page, hidden on narrow screens. Options: "10 entries per page" (default), "20 entries per page", "30 entries per page", "50 entries per page", "100 entries per page".

> **[TABLE · data]** Every SWAYAM-NPTEL Local Chapter college, one row each, in the order returned by the database (about 8,800 rows, about 880 pages at 10 per page). The college name is a link to https://nptel.ac.in/localchapter/statistics/COLLEGE ID (opens in a new tab). Status shows a green "Active" for active chapters, a red bold "Not Active" for inactive ones, and is empty otherwise. The Address cell is the first address line, city and pincode joined as "ADDRESS CITY-PINCODE". If the search matches nothing, one row reads "No Data Found". While the data loads the page reads "Loading...". Column headings:
>
> | College Name | LC Id | Status | Address | State | SPOC Name | Co-ordinated by |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

> **[PAGINATION]** Previous and next arrow icons (alt text "previous page button" and "next page button") with numbered page buttons between them: the current page and its neighbours, the first page and "..." when past page 4, and "..." and the last page when more than three pages remain.

#### Rating of Local Chapter

> **[QUERY STRING]** Shown for https://nptel.ac.in/localchapter/details?page=ratings. The list tab's content is kept in the page but hidden while this tab is open.

> **[TABS]** One tab per year, 2026 (open by default) down to 2016. On narrow screens the year buttons are replaced by a drop-down list with the same years. Each year's content is shown below under its year.
>
> | 2026 | 2025 | 2024 | 2023 | 2022 | 2021 | 2020 | 2019 | 2018 | 2017 | 2016 |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | 

> **[LINK CARDS]** Under each year, the links are cards with a yellow left border; each opens in a new tab.
