>
> | SPOC Details | Course Wise | Toppers Details |

#### SPOC Details

> **[COLLEGE CARD · data]** The same college card as on https://nptel.ac.in/localchapter/statistics/COLLEGE ID: college logo (IMAGE), college name, address, "Url: " and the college website link, SPOC photo (IMAGE), then "SPOC Name - ", "Designation - ", "Partnering since - ", "College Id - " and " Institute Type - " each followed by its value, and an onboard badge IMAGE when the SPOC is onboarded.

> **[BANNER · data]** Yellow and grey banner, shown only when the college has a rating for this semester. One line: the semester (for example "Jan-Apr 2026: ") followed by "CONGRATS! You are one of the top 200 Local Chapters." and "Your college is hereby recognized as an ACTIVE Local Chapter." for A, AA or AAA; "Your college is hereby recognized as an ACTIVE Local Chapter." for Active; or "Your college is hereby recognized as an RATING IN CAPITALS Local Chapter." for Active - Aspirants, Active - Contenders and Active - Prospects.

> **[TABLE · data]** One row for this semester, with the semester name, the counts for the college and its rating. Empty values show "-". The NPTEL Stars cell is a "Details" link to https://nptel.ac.in/localchapter/lcstars/COLLEGE ID/SEMESTER CODE (opens in a new tab). Column headings:
>
> | Course Run | Present | Gold | Elite | Silver | Sucessful | Participation | Topper | Rating | NPTEL Stars |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

#### Course Wise

> **[TABLE · data]** One row per course in which the college had learners present in this semester (about 100 rows for a large chapter, in the order returned by the database; no search or paging). Columns are the serial number, the course name and the counts. If there are none, one row reads "No course data available for this semester." Column headings:
>
> | Sr No | Course Name | Present | Gold | Elite | Silver | Sucessful | Participation |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

#### Toppers Details

> **[GRID · data]** Cards, one per topper from the college in this semester (about 15 cards). Each card shows the learner's photo (IMAGE), the learner's name, the final score as a percentage, a line "Top N% of certified candidates", and the course name. If there are none, the page reads "No toppers data available for this semester."
