#### Believers

NPTEL BELIEVERS

Present in 4/5/6 exams with passing atleast 4 exams appeared for

#### Discipline stars

NPTEL Discipline Stars

Certified in courses of same discipline, more than 50 weeks of learning, final score in each subject >= 55.

> **[GRID · data]** "Total Candidates: N" followed by one card per learner, with the same photo, name, role and college name as above. The coloured top strip reads "Max Weeks: N" instead of "Courses taken".

#### Domain scholars

NPTEL Domain Scholars

Complete core/elective courses, minimum score>=55 in each course with average of 60, complete weeks required

> **[GRID · data]** "Total Candidates: N" followed by one card per learner (strip "Courses taken: N", then photo, name, role and college name).

#### Enthusiasts

NPTEL Enthusiasts

Appeared in atleast 8 exams in 4 semesters, passed in 75% of courses appeared (not looking for continuity here).

> **[GRID · data]** "Total Candidates: N" followed by one card per learner (strip "Courses taken: N", then photo, name, role and college name).

#### Evangelists

NPTEL Evangelists

Present in atleast half of the exams conducted in last 4 years/8 semesters

> **[GRID · data]** "Total Candidates: N" followed by one card per learner (strip "Courses taken: N", then photo, name, role and college name).

#### Motivated Learners

NPTEL Motivated Learners

Appeared in atleast 8 exams in 4 semesters, passed in 75% of courses appeared, atleast one exam in each of the 4 semesters.

> **[GRID · data]** "Total Candidates: N" followed by one card per learner (strip "Courses taken: N", then photo, name, role and college name).

#### Superstars

NPTEL Superstars

Appeared in 4/5/6 exams and topper in atleast 3 exams.

> **[GRID · data]** "Total Candidates: N" followed by one card per learner (strip "Courses taken: N", then photo, name, role and college name).
