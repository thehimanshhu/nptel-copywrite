## About Industry Associate

**National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning (NPTEL)** is an MoE project which was initiated in 2003 to provide quality education for all those who have the interest to learn. Since 2013 NPTEL has been providing online certification through video lectures on various domains. We are now looking into partnering with industries to explore ways by which we can bridge the gap between the academics and the industry and also create courses along with the industry to cross-skill and up-skill the existing workforce. CSR initiatives are also welcomed as part of this association. For a detailed understanding of this partnership read through the **[MoU](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zDekFGhXwX4XkI9XnWnaThGz1ihiegFP/view)** diligently.

For further queries please mail to **niasupport@nptel.iitm.ac.in.** Our team will get in touch with you.

## Why Partner With NPTEL?

Why Partner With NPTEL?

1. **Online courses to learn for free** - Videos & text can be accessed for FREE from nptel.ac.in. Provision for copying up to 15 TB of NPTEL course videos to your hard drive is available FREE of cost.

2. **Recruit Interns/Fresh hires** - NPTEL would act as a liaison between the Industries and Academia. Give learners exposure to the current market trends, while connecting the Industries to the best skillset.

3. **Fresher Onboarding** - NPTEL has several courses which can be used by companies in training freshers, in different skill sets. Courses such as Enhancing Soft Skills and Personality, Programming, Data Structures and Algorithms, Database Management System and Business English Communication can be used in this regard.

4. **Upskill Workforce** - Some of our advanced courses can be used to upskill employees. These are a few of the sample courses.
   1. Artificial Intelligence
   2. Design for Internet Of Things
   3. Introduction to Machine Learning
   4. Electric Vehicles
   5. VLSI Design Verification and test
   6. Networks and Systems
   7. Design of Steel Structures
   8. Industrial Automation and Control
   9. Total Quality Management

5. **Co-offer Courses** - Experts from companies can co-offer courses along with IIT faculty which can facilitate both existing employees, new recruits as well as interested learners.
