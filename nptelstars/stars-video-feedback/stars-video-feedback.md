> **[BREADCRUMB]** NPTEL > [Stars](https://nptel.ac.in/nptelstars) > NPTEL Stars Video Feedbacks. There is no page heading below it.

> **[SELECT]** Star category dropdown, defaults to NPTEL Domain Scholars. Options, in order: NPTEL Domain Scholars, NPTEL Superstars, NPTEL Evangelists, NPTEL Motivated Learners, NPTEL Enthusiasts, NPTEL Discipline Stars, NPTEL Believers. Choosing one changes which videos are shown below.

> **[VIDEO · data]** Grid of embedded YouTube video players, one per learner who recorded a feedback video for the selected category (about 3 to 13 per category). The list is loaded from a data file. Under each player: the learner's name, role and college, which are not reproduced here.
