# NPTEL Believers

> **[SELECT]** Semester dropdown, defaults to the first option (Jan-Apr 2026). Options, in order: Jan-Apr 2026, Jul-Dec 2025, Jan-Apr 2025, Jul-Dec 2024, Jan-Apr 2024, Jul-Dec 2023, Jan-Apr 2023, Jul-Dec 2022, Jan-Apr 2022, Jul-Dec 2021, Jan-Apr 2021, Jan-Dec 2020, Jul-Dec 2019. Changing it updates the semester named in the criteria text and the list of learners.

Candidates will be recognized as NPTEL BELIEVERS if

1. They were present for 4/5/6/7 exams in Jan-Apr 2026.
2. They were certified in at least 4 exams in which they have appeared.

Please refer to the [document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQJIK6JKUseX58jtfxY_LI_lKt9_hIi_YMsdxa5Ww9zMLn4K9zwgJ9dYZv3NGfjKYFymBDD3JIgUN26/pub) for more information on STARS.

> **[GRID · data]** Learner cards for the selected semester, sorted by courses taken, then toppers, then name. Each card has a coloured top band showing "Courses taken: N", the learner's photo, name, role (student / faculty / employed / other) and college. Above the cards: "Total Candidates: N" (about 2900 for the default semester). Learner names, photos and colleges are not reproduced here.
