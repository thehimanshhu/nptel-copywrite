### Conferences

#### NPTEL Conference Sponsorship Support

As part of its ongoing efforts to promote academic excellence and research dissemination, NPTEL offers Conference Sponsorship Support to Local Chapter SPOCs/faculty members and NPTEL STARs. The initiative aims to provide opportunities for faculty and students to present high-quality research at reputed national conferences, while fostering collaboration, innovation, and professional growth.

##### Key Objectives

* Encourage the presentation and dissemination of high-quality research at recognized national conferences.
* Support capacity building and professional development among faculty members and students.
* Foster academic collaboration, knowledge exchange, and research networking.
* Promote institutional visibility and strengthen research outreach across academic and professional platforms.
* Provide NPTEL learners and faculty with greater exposure to emerging developments and opportunities within their respective domains.

##### Eligibility and Conditions

Applicants seeking NPTEL Conference Sponsorship Support must meet the following criteria:

* The applicant must be a Local Chapter SPOC/faculty member or an NPTEL STAR.
* The research paper must have been accepted for oral or poster presentation at a recognized national conference.
* The applicant must be the primary author or presenting author of the accepted paper.
* The application must be endorsed by the applicant’s institution through the designated authority, such as the Head of Institution or Local Chapter SPOC.
* Applicants must not have received NPTEL Conference Sponsorship Support during the same academic year.
* All participants receiving NPTEL sponsorship must acknowledge NPTEL’s support in the presentation, publication, or any other material associated with the sponsored conference.

##### Sponsorship Coverage

The NPTEL Conference Sponsorship Support may cover the following expenses:

* Conference registration fee.
* Travel and accommodation expenses, as per the applicable organizational norms.
* The final sponsorship amount sanctioned will be determined based on the nature and relevance of the conference, conference location, and availability of funds.

##### Application Process

Applicants seeking conference sponsorship must follow the process outlined below:

1) Submit the online sponsorship application form with the required details.
2) Upload all supporting documents in PDF format.
3) Submit a copy of the accepted abstract or full paper, along with the conference acceptance notification.
4) The submitted applications will be reviewed and evaluated by the Central Committee.
5) Applicants will be informed of the decision by email following completion of the review process.

#### Frequently asked questions

##### General Queries

**What is the purpose of NPTEL providing support for conference participation?**

NPTEL aims to empower SPOCs and STARs by providing professional development opportunities through sponsorship for participation in reputed academic and technical conferences. Such events facilitate networking, exposure to emerging research and technologies, and interaction with experts in the field. The knowledge and insights gained through these engagements are expected to benefit the participants’ home institutions while further strengthening the NPTEL community.

**Who identifies the conferences to attend?**

SPOCs/STARs are expected to identify relevant conferences within their respective disciplines. NPTEL does not maintain or recommend a fixed list of conferences. However, the selected conference must meet NPTEL’s prescribed guidelines with respect to the venue and academic quality.

**Why are the supported conference venues restricted?**

Support is currently extended only to conferences hosted by reputed institutions such as IITs, IISc, IISERs, IIST, NITs, and CSIR laboratories, to ensure academic credibility and relevance. Conferences organized by Management and Humanities departments within these institutions are also eligible for support.

**What is the significance of the IEEE Conference on Technology for Education (T4E)?**

The IEEE T4E Conference focuses on innovations in teaching, learning, and education technology. It is a recommended forum for SPOCs/STARs interested in sharing research or experiences related to technology-enabled learning. Submissions typically open in July, with results announced by August–September.

**Are non-teaching SPOCs eligible for this support?**

Yes. Non-teaching staff serving as SPOCs are also fully eligible to avail themselves of NPTEL’s conference support initiative.

**Can another faculty member attend the conference on behalf of the SPOC?**

No. The sponsorship is non-transferable and can only be availed by the SPOC for whom the support has been approved.

**Are faculty members from the Local Chapters (other than the SPOC) eligible?**

Yes. They may avail the support, provided they obtain approval from the Head of the Institution and apply through the SPOC, who must submit the conference support form on their behalf.

**How many conferences can be supported per SPOC/STAR in a year?**

Each SPOC/STAR can receive support for one conference per academic year.

**Is there a monthly or semester-wise limit on the number of requests supported?**

Yes. Applications are reviewed monthly, and only a limited number of SPOCs/STARs are sponsored in each cycle, depending on available funds and merit.

**Is the support applicable for a limited time period?**

Yes. The initiative is reviewed annually, and its continuation depends on the outcomes and feedback from supported participants.

**How and when is the support amount disbursed?**

The approved amount is reimbursed after participation. SPOCs/STARs must submit all relevant bills, certificates of attendance, and required documentation for the reimbursement.

**What expenses are covered under this support?**

The financial support covers:

  * Conference registration fees
  * Round-trip travel expenses
  * Accommodation expenses during the conference

The total support is capped at ₹25,000. Personal expenses and expenses unrelated to the conference are not eligible for reimbursement.

**Can SPOCs/STARs choose any topic for their conference paper?**

Yes, as long as it aligns with the conference’s theme and is accepted through its peer-review process. NPTEL does not impose topic restrictions.

##### Queries Related to the Application Form

**Where can SPOCs find their College LC ID?**

The LC ID can be found on the official NPTEL Local Chapter page. A direct link is provided in the application form.

**How do I enter the conference website URL?**

Enter the complete and correct URL of the conference homepage. Avoid incomplete or shortened links.

**Do I need to acknowledge NPTEL’s support?**

Yes. All sponsored participants must acknowledge NPTEL’s support in their presentation, paper, or report.

**Is a post-conference report required?**

Yes. SPOCs must submit a brief report summarizing key learnings, professional outcomes, and the overall benefit to their institution and the NPTEL community.
