# NPTEL Evangelists

> **[SELECT]** Semester dropdown, defaults to the first option (Jul-Dec 2025). Options, in order: Jul-Dec 2025, Jan-Dec 2024, Jan-Dec 2023, Jan-Dec 2022, Jan-Dec 2021, Jan-Dec 2020, Jan-Dec 2019. Changing it updates the semester named in the opening sentence, adds a bracketed list of years after the first criterion for older semesters, and updates the list of learners.

Candidates who satisfy the following conditions will be the NPTEL EVANGELISTS in Jul-Dec 2025.

NPTEL Evangelists title is awarded annually based on the following criteria. Eligible candidates will receive their certificates in December each year.

1. We consider the last 4 years/8 semesters.
2. The total number of exams a candidate could have taken for is calculated as: **(Sum of exam dates in each year) × 2** (to account for two shifts per exam date).
3. Candidates must have appeared in at least **50% of the total exams** conducted over the last 4 years.
4. Candidates must have successfully passed at least **2/3 of the exams applied for.**

Please refer to the [document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQJIK6JKUseX58jtfxY_LI_lKt9_hIi_YMsdxa5Ww9zMLn4K9zwgJ9dYZv3NGfjKYFymBDD3JIgUN26/pub) for more information on STARS.

> **[GRID · data]** Learner cards for the selected semester, sorted by courses taken, then toppers, then name. Each card has a coloured top band showing "Courses taken: N", the learner's photo, name, role (student / faculty / employed / other) and college. Above the cards: "Total Candidates: N" (about 12 for the default semester). Learner names, photos and colleges are not reproduced here.
