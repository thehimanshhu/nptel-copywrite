# NPTEL Enthusiasts

> **[SELECT]** Semester dropdown, defaults to the first option (Jan-Apr 2026). Options, in order: Jan-Apr 2026, Jul-Dec 2025, Jan-Apr 2025, Jul-Dec 2024, Jan-Apr 2024, Jul-Dec 2023, Jan-Apr 2023, Jul-Dec 2022, Jan-Apr 2022, Jul-Dec 2021, Jan-Apr 2021, Jan-Dec 2020, Jul-Dec 2019. Changing it updates the semester named in the opening sentence, the four semesters in brackets in the first criterion, and the list of learners.

Candidates who satisfy the following conditions are the NPTEL ENTHUSIASTS in Jan-Apr 2026.

1. NPTEL learner must have been present for 8 or more NPTEL exams in the last 4 semesters (July 2024, Jan 2025, July 2025, Jan 2026).
2. The learner must have passed in 75% or more of the exams appeared for. (For eg: if he/she has appeared for 12 exams, he/she should have passed in 9 courses atleast).

Please refer to the [document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQJIK6JKUseX58jtfxY_LI_lKt9_hIi_YMsdxa5Ww9zMLn4K9zwgJ9dYZv3NGfjKYFymBDD3JIgUN26/pub) for more information on STARS.

> **[GRID · data]** Learner cards for the selected semester, sorted by courses taken, then toppers, then name. Each card has a coloured top band showing "Courses taken: N", the learner's photo, name, role (student / faculty / employed / other) and college. Above the cards: "Total Candidates: N" (about 1100 for the default semester). Learner names, photos and colleges are not reproduced here.
