# NPTEL Discipline Stars

> **[SELECT]** Semester dropdown, defaults to the first option (Jan-Apr 2026). Options, in order: Jan-Apr 2026, Jul-Dec 2025, Jan-Apr 2025, Jul-Dec 2024, Jan-Apr 2024, Jul-Dec 2023, Jan-Apr 2023, Jul-Dec 2022, Jan-Apr 2022, Jul-Dec 2021, Jan-Apr 2021, Jan-Dec 2020, Jul-Dec 2019. Changing it updates the list of learners; the criteria text does not change.

> **[SELECT]** Discipline dropdown, defaults to Aerospace Engineering. Options, in order: Aerospace Engineering, Biotechnology, Chemical Engineering, Chemistry and Biochemistry, Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Engineering Design, Electrical Engineering, Humanities and Social Sciences, Management, Mathematics, Mechanical Engineering, Metallurgy and Material Science, Ocean Engineering, Physics, Textile Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Nanotechnology, Atmospheric Science, Environmental Science, General, Basic courses(Sem 1 and 2), Mining Engineering, Architecture, Automobile Engineering, Agriculture, Multidisciplinary, Special Lecture Series, Law, Economics. Choosing one filters the cards.

1. Candidates should have completed courses whose duration totals to 50 weeks or more.
2. All courses should be in a particular discipline (as seen from the discipline of the course code).
3. The final score in the course certificate must be >=55.

Please refer to the [document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQJIK6JKUseX58jtfxY_LI_lKt9_hIi_YMsdxa5Ww9zMLn4K9zwgJ9dYZv3NGfjKYFymBDD3JIgUN26/pub) for more information on STARS.

**Note:** If a candidate has already received the NPTEL Discipline Stars certificate once, they will be eligible to claim it again only if they have completed courses totalling 50 weeks or more in duration, with each course having a final score of 55 or above, starting from the semester next to which they last received the certificate.

Please note that the discipline mapping will be based strictly on the classification provided on the SWAYAM platform for the respective semester during which the candidate took the course. No alternate or retrospective discipline tagging will be considered.

> **[GRID · data]** Learner cards for the selected semester, sorted by maximum weeks, then courses taken, then toppers, then name. Each card has a coloured top band showing "Max Weeks: N", the learner's photo, name, role (student / faculty / employed / other) and college. Above the cards: "Total Candidates: N" (about 13 for the default semester). Learner names, photos and colleges are not reproduced here.
