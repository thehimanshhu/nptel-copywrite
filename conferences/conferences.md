# Conferences from IITs, IISc and partner institutes

To explore information on upcoming research conferences from IITs, IISc and partner institutes, please click registration page.

**[Browse conferences](https://nptel.ac.in/conferences#conference-results)** *(button)*

> **[GRID · data]** Summary strip of four counts, computed from the conference list: Total listed | Upcoming | Departments | Support available. Labels: Total listed, Upcoming, Departments, Support available (a number appears beside each).

## Find conferences

> **[FILTER PANEL]** Left-hand filter panel. Every filter narrows the cards on the right immediately; nothing is submitted. A **Reset** button clears them all.
>
> **[Reset](https://nptel.ac.in/conferences)** *(button)*
>
> Search by name, department or venue *(text box, placeholder "Optimization, Chennai, Physics...")*
>
> Department *(dropdown; first option "All departments", then each department found in the list, alphabetical, about 28 options)*
>
> Month *(dropdown; first option "All months", then one option per month that has a conference, in date order)*
>
> From *(date picker)*   To *(date picker)*
>
> Status *(button group, "All" selected by default)*: All | Upcoming | On-going | Completed

## Conference cards

> **[GRID · data]** About 66 conference cards, loaded from a data file kept with the site's code (not live database data). Above the cards: "N matching conferences. Open a card for more details and registration." and a sort dropdown: Sort: Active first (default) | Sort: Soonest date | Sort: Department | Sort: Conference name.
>
> Each card shows:
>
> * a status badge: Upcoming, On-going or Completed, and, for some conferences, a second badge "Conference supported by NPTEL"
> * conference title (heading)
> * "Department: …"
> * "Institute: …"
> * "Venue: …"
> * "Date: …" (a date range)
> * **[More details](https://nptel.ac.in/conferences)** *(button)* opening the conference's own registration page in a new tab (link target differs per conference)
>
> When no card matches the filters: heading "No conferences match these filters.", text "Try removing one filter, changing the month, or searching by a broader department or venue." and a **Reset filters** button.
