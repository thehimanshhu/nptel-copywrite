### Ansys Innovation Courses (AIC) - Offer for NPTEL Learners

#### Free track completion badge

#### Eligibility:

1. NPTEL learners of Elite/Silver/Gold/Topper category of [20 selected NPTEL courses](https://courses.ansys.com/index.php/mapping-aic-nptel-courses/) will be awarded with a free track completion badge worth 12,000 INR on completion of the associated mapped Ansys track of courses.
2. Please fill out the interest form - [https://forms.gle/XNebErybBXpdms6S9](https://forms.gle/XNebErybBXpdms6S9)
3. Eligible NPTEL Elite learners will be provided a coupon by NPTEL to earn a free badge after submission of the screenshot of Ansys Track completion.

#### Product and benefits:

1. Digital badge contains verified metadata that describes user participation in the specific learning track consisting of multiple courses.
2. A track completion digital badge allows user to showcase his or her expertise and get recognized globally via online platforms.
3. Digital badges can be used in email signatures, digital resumes, social and professional media.

#### How to Earn free digital badge:

1. Once the coupon is received students need to visit: [Ansys Learning Tracks](https://courses.ansys.com/index.php/learning-tracks/)
2. Select the designated track from available Learning Tracks.
3. Complete all the courses associated with the track along with the quiz.
4. Click on the Buy Badge option on the Learning Track page.
5. Complete the transaction by entering the coupon code provided.

#### Recognition by posting on professional network:

1. Leverage the [workflow](https://courses.ansys.com/index.php/learning-track-workflow/) to post the digital badge on professional network.
2. Tag the Ansys Learning page on LinkedIn when posting for a chance to win a prize.
