# NPTEL Summer/ Winter Internship

> **[SECTION]** Home, selected by default

### About Us

NPTEL provides an opportunity to its learners to have research experiences with the faculty from IITs/IISc/IISER/IIIT/other course offering institutes.

* NPTEL internship opportunities are available under the supervision of the course instructors at the course-offering institutes.
* The internships are generally offered during the summer (May/Jun/Jul) and winter (Nov/Dec/Jan) semester breaks depending on the availability of resources.
* The eligible candidates of Jan-Apr semester will be considered for the winter internships, and the eligible candidates of Jul-Oct semesters for the summer internship opportunity.

#### PRE INTERNSHIP

#### Eligibility

* NPTEL course toppers and candidates passing the certification exam in the Elite-Gold category are only eligible to apply.
* The internships will be considered for the Students and Faculty members (as per the profession selected during the course enrolment).
* The students will be considered for the in-person internships only. Faculty members who cannot avail leave from their workplace and the candidates with disabilities (PwD) will only be considered for the virtual internship, wherever feasible.

#### Duration of internship

* Internships can be offered for a period of 2/4/6/8/10/12 weeks duration. The duration will be decided by the course instructor based on the nature of internship related work and the availability of other logistics.
* The tentative start date of internship can be adjusted based on the mutual convenience of the selected candidates and the faculty.

#### Application Process and Selection

* The NPTEL office will contact the course instructor to check if they can take interns from the toppers/Elite-Gold category of learners of their respective courses.
* Depending on the availability and confirmation from the course instructor, the eligible candidates of those courses where the consent of the faculty has been obtained will be informed through email with the detailed application process and the internship schedule.
* The NPTEL will send the communication in this regard by April for the Summer internships and by October for the Winter internships.
* The interested eligible candidates are expected to check their academic calendar, get the consent of their college authorities and apply online with the following documents
  * Resume
  * Last 2-semester grade cards
  * Statement of purpose
  * NPTEL course certificate
* The selection of the candidate will be made by the course instructor based on the documents submitted/personal interview/any other means as it is deemed suitable.
* The decision of the faculty on the selection process is final and binding on all.
* The selected candidates will be issued with the offer letters copying their college authorities.

#### Accommodation

NPTEL will try to arrange for your accommodation at one of the hostels within the institute premises. The local institute guidelines in this regard are binding and the availability of hostel accommodation cannot be guaranteed. This factor has to be taken into consideration while applying for the internships.

In case of non-availability of in-campus hostel accommodation, the candidate has to arrange for his/her own accommodation outside. The local NPTEL offices at IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Kanpur, IIT Kharagpur, IIT Madras, IIT Roorkee, and IISc Bangalore can assist you in finding suitable outside accommodation, if required. The contact details of the local offices will be provided in the internship offer letters.

For NPTEL internships at institutes other than those IITs/IISc mentioned above, the selected candidates have to get in touch with the Professor (through official email/phone) for any support or make their own arrangements.

#### Stipend

The stipend for the in-person NPTEL internship has been revised effective from Summer Internship 2025 as per the following details. No stipends are payable for the virtual internship.

| Duration | Stipend amount |

The stipend will be payable on successful completion of the internship.

> **[TAB CONTENT]** DURING THE INTERNSHIP

* The selected candidate has to contact the local NPTEL office/course instructor as soon as the offer letter is received for further guidance on booking of accommodation. The booking of hostel accommodation is not automatic and the candidate has to fill in the required forms with documents for approval.
* On the first day of internship, the candidate has to report to the local NPTEL office (for internships at IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Kanpur, IIT Kharagpur, IIT Madras, IIT Roorkee, and IISc Bangalore) and submit their joining report. For internships at other institutes, an email confirming joining the internship has to be sent to nptel-internship@nptel.iitm.ac.in copying the course instructor.
* Subsequently, they will have to mandatorily mark their daily attendance through their login in the https://nptel.iitm.ac.in (to be activated when the internship starts) during their entire period of internships. This is irrespective of whether the candidate is making attendance locally or attending internship virtually.

> **[TAB CONTENT]** POST INTERNSHIP

* On successful completion of the internship, the candidate has to submit the final report to the Professor for approval. The approval can be accorded by the supervisor by directly signing on the report, issuing a separate letter in this regard either in official letterhead or through email confirmation.
* The approved report is to be shared with the NPTEL office along with a passport size photograph of the candidates. The banking and PAN details of the candidate will be required for stipend processing. The detailed procedure in this regard will be communicated to the candidates through email.
* The certificate will be issued to the candidates based on the regularity of the candidate and the feedback received from the course instructor and stipend processed.

### FAQ

#### When will Internships be open?

Internships are offered in summer (May/June/July) and winter (Nov/Dec/Jan) each year.

#### How do I apply for the internship?

Each semester, after the results are published for NPTEL Online Certification courses, we contact the toppers of select courses regarding the internship. You will be given instructions on how to apply at that time.

#### What documents do I need to submit?

Your resume, NPTEL Course Certificate, a one-page summary of the research proposal/ statement of purpose, and last two - semester mark sheets.

#### Who is eligible for applying for the internship?

Candidates who are top performers in the Nptel Online Certification Courses may be eligible for an internship offer. The final decision is made by the course instructor.

#### How would I come to know if my application has been accepted or not?

We shall contact you via mail / call once we get the confirmation from the Professor.

#### Once internship is offered, what are the formalities involved?

On the first day of internship, you have to report at the local NPTEL office, fill up the registration form, and then meet with the concerned course instructor.

#### Who do I report to?

During your internship, you are expected to report to the local NPTEL office and to the course instructor as per his/her directions.

#### What will be the duration of the internship?

The internship duration could be for 2, 3 or 4 weeks. The internship will at the concerned faculty's institute premises.

#### If I am interested in extending the Internship, is it allowed?

This is a decision that will be made by the concerned faculty.

#### Will I get any stipend for this internship?

Stipend depends on the duration of the internship. 2wk - Rs. 2500, 4wk - Rs. 5000, 6wk - Rs. 7500, 8wk - Rs. 10,000 Stipend will be paid only those who successfully complete the internship. Stipend will be deposited in the bank a/c number provided by the intern.

#### After completion of the internship will I be given a Certificate?

Yes, after completion of the internship certificate would be provided from NPTEL

#### Will accommodation and travel arrangements be taken care of by NPTEL?

Hostel accommodation and logistics will be arranged by us depending on availability. Travel arrangement has to be done by you.

#### After completion, what are the formalities?

Intern has to fill a feedback form and also give us a brief report of the internship experience. We also collect feedback from the faculty. This report is made available on our portal [https://nptel.ac.in/internship/](https://nptel.ac.in/internship)
