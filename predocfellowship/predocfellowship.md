### About Us

**Launch Your Research Career with NPTEL Predoctoral Fellowships!**

NPTEL is excited to announce the NPTEL Pre-Doctoral Fellowships program for outstanding NPTEL learners with a Bachelor's or Master's degree! This program is designed to bridge the gap between online learning and research experience, preparing you for success in postgraduate studies.

#### Timeline:

* **Cycle 1:** August 2024 - August 2025 [Completed]
* **Cycle 2:** July 2025 - July 2026 [Ongoing]
* **Cycle 3:** The application form for Pre-Doc Fellowship 2026 (Cycle 3) is now open. The application link has been shared via email with eligible candidates. Last date for form submission: March 31, 2026

#### Program Details

* **Eligibility:** Open to all NPTEL Stars with a Bachelor's or Master's degree
* **Duration:** Up to 1 year
* **Total Fellowship Amount:** ₹4,00,000 per year, inclusive of the monthly stipend and contingency grant.
  * **Monthly Stipend:** ₹25,000+HRA per month for a duration of 12 months.
  * **Contingency Grant:** An additional ₹1,00,000 per year for research-related expenses.
* **Location:** In-person at the institute where you are selected for Fellowship
* **Project Selection:** Up to 1 year
  * Each institute will advertise faculty-led research projects and academic requirements.
  * You can apply to up to five projects, ranking your preferences.
  * Faculty will select students based on resumes, short videos, and interviews, prioritizing your preferences.
* **Important Note**
  * The fellowship period is for one (1) year, contingent upon satisfactory performance and conduct of the student, as determined by the Institute.
  * This fellowship is designed to provide a pathway to pursue advanced research opportunities and does not guarantee admission to M.S or Ph.D programs in the concerned institute or under the faculty.
* **Registration details**
  * The registration period for Cycle 2 has already concluded.

#### Benefits

* Gain valuable research experience under renowned faculty.
* Prepare for postgraduate studies (Master's or PhD) at a top institute.
* Network with leading researchers in your field.
* Receive a competitive monthly stipend.

#### Accommodation and Travel Allowance

* On-campus housing is not provided. You will be responsible for securing your own accommodation.
* Travel allowance is not included.

#### Application Process

* Await the announcement of available projects and academic requirements.
* Review the project listings and submit your application, ranking your top five choices.
* Shortlisted candidates will be contacted for interviews.
* Upon selection, you will receive an offer letter and confirm your start date within the program timeframe.

## General Information

###### What is the NPTEL Post Baccalaureate/Pre Doc Fellowships program?

The NPTEL Post Baccalaureate/Pre Doc Fellowships program is designed to provide recent graduates and early-career researchers with the opportunity to conduct advanced research under the guidance of experienced faculty members.

###### Who is eligible to apply for the fellowship?

The fellowship is open to all NPTEL Stars who have a bachelor's degree in a relevant field, a strong academic record, and a demonstrated interest in research. Specific eligibility requirements may vary depending on the project.

###### What is the duration of the fellowship?

The fellowship typically lasts for one year.

> **[SECTION]** Shown only when "Application Process" is selected in the FAQ side menu.

## Application Process

###### How do I apply for the fellowship?

Applications can be submitted online through our official website. You will need to fill out an application form and submit required documents such as your resume, academic transcripts, and a statement of purpose. Please note that the application is currently closed.

###### What documents are required for the application?

Required documents generally include a resume/CV, academic transcripts, letters of recommendation, and a statement of purpose. Specific projects may have additional requirements.

###### What is the mode of the program?

The program is conducted in-person at one of the eight prestigious partner institutes (IIT Bombay, Delhi, Guwahati, Kanpur, Kharagpur, Madras, Roorkee, and IISc Bangalore)

###### Is there an application fee?

Yes, there is an application fee of Rs. 500 for the fellowship program.

> **[SECTION]** Shown only when "Fellowship Details" is selected in the FAQ side menu.

## Fellowship Details

###### What is the stipend amount for the fellowship?

The fellowship offers a stipend of ₹25,000 per month, disbursed on a monthly basis.

###### Are accommodation and travel allowances provided?

The institute does not provide on-campus housing, and fellows are responsible for securing their own accommodation. Travel allowances are not provided.

###### What are the expectations for fellowship recipients?

Fellows are expected to conduct high-quality research, regularly meet with their faculty advisor, and submit progress reports. Requirements will vary from project to project.

> **[SECTION]** Shown only when "During the Fellowship" is selected in the FAQ side menu.

## During the Fellowship

###### Can the fellowship be terminated early?

The fellowship period is contingent upon satisfactory performance and conduct. It may be terminated at any point by the Faculty Advisor due to unsatisfactory performance or misconduct.

###### Will I have opportunities to publish my research?

Opportunities for publishing research should be discussed with your faculty coordinator once you are selected for the project.

###### What kind of support will I receive during the fellowship?

Fellows will receive guidance from their faculty advisor, access to research facilities, and opportunities for professional development.

> **[SECTION]** Shown only when "Post-Fellowship" is selected in the FAQ side menu.

## Post-Fellowship

###### Does this fellowship guarantee admission to an MS or Ph.D. program?

This fellowship is focused on providing research experience and does not have a direct link with MS or Ph.D. admissions at the institute or under the faculty advisor.

###### What career opportunities are available after the fellowship?

Alumni of the fellowship program often pursue advanced degrees or careers in academia, industry, or research institutions.
